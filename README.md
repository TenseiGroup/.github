# Tensei Group

**Tensei (転生)** is a Japanese word for rebirth — the transformation of one
existence into another. For us, it is a simple belief: technology can give
ideas, businesses, and entire systems a new life.

Tensei Group is a technology group building the next generation of products,
systems, and companies — from Africa, for the world.

We are early. This organization is where we build.

## Focus

- **Tensei Security** — cybersecurity products and services
- **Tensei Labs** — AI research, emerging technology, experimental products
- **Tensei Systems** — enterprise software and infrastructure
- **Tensei Cloud** — cloud and developer infrastructure
- **Tensei Ventures** — future investment arm

## Principles

- **Build** — craftsmanship first
- **Transform** — take what exists and give it a new life
- **Repeat** — long-term thinking, each cycle raising the bar
